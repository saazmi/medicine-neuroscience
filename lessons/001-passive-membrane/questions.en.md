# Exercises

*Version française (référence) : [questions.md](questions.md).*

Original questions based on the stated model. Answer before viewing the [solutions](solutions.en.md). No treatment decisions are involved.

1. **Meaning.** At $V=E_L$, what is the leak current? What is the initial slope after switching on $I_0$?
2. **Derivation.** Solve the ODE for constant $I_0$ and arbitrary $V(0)=V_0$.
3. **Units.** Explain why MΩ × pF gives microseconds before accounting for the numerical coefficients.
4. **Graph.** In panel A, which curve changes fastest initially? Does its final amplitude differ?
5. **Transfer.** Double $R_m$ while keeping $C_m$ and $I_0$ fixed. What happens to the time constant, final change, and initial slope?
6. **Synthetic experiment.** A 40 pA step produces a 4 mV steady change; the time constant is 15 ms. Estimate $R_m$ and $C_m$.
7. **Model boundary.** Can the calculated voltage curve prove that a biological neuron will fire? Explain.
8. **Extension.** Derive the magnitude and phase of $Z(\omega)$ and the frequency where magnitude is $1/\sqrt{2}$ of its zero-frequency value.

Assessment suggestion: score 1–7 out of two points each (result + reasoning). Track question 8 separately as mathematical enrichment. Repeat with changed parameters after a delay. This is an original practice set, not a validated medical-school exam.
